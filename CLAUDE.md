## Standards & Conventions

### Excel standards (always follow)

- **Inputs, calculations and outputs on separate sheets**, or in clearly marked areas.
- **No hard-coded numbers inside formulas.** Every assumption goes in a
  labelled input cell.
- Consistent colour coding: **blue font = inputs**, **black = formulas**,
  **green = links to other sheets**.
- One formula per row, copied across consistently. No breaks in a formula
  pattern.
- Keep formulas simple and auditable. Prefer helper columns over deeply nested
  formulas. Use `XLOOKUP`/`INDEX-MATCH`, not `VLOOKUP`.
- Include **check cells** (e.g. balance sheet balances, totals reconcile),
  with a summary of all checks on the front sheet.
- Add a **cover/notes sheet** with: purpose, client, period, version, author,
  key assumptions, and the sources of all data.
- No macros/VBA unless I ask for them.
- Use the same units throughout (e.g. £/$ whole units or thousands), and state
  the unit on every sheet.

## Rules: Always / Never

**Always**
- Recalculate and check totals before handing anything over.
- Clearly list every assumption you make.
- Flag anomalies, missing data and possible errors. Do not quietly "fix" them.
- Ask me before making a judgement call (treatments, estimates, materiality).

**Never**
- Contact a client or send anything externally without my explicit approval.
- Invent figures or fill gaps with made-up data.
- Share or reuse one client's information in another client's work.
- Present an output as final or reviewed.
